Assessing the cardiac condition and building a statistical model for classification is an important step to identify the detection of accurate cases. In this paper, the Logistic Regression model 
is applied on the cardiac condition dataset and the performance on the model is evaluated with the test data. F1 scores, Precision and Recall accuracy and balanced accuracy score is derived and is
checked for the final model. This paper also implements a time series forecasting on weather dataset on how the parameter- maximum air temperature in degree Celsius, will be forecasted using different
time series technique and identify the model that closely forecast the temperature based on the underlying patterns between the year 2019 and 2022 to forecast the temperature for 2023. A logistic model
is built with an F1 score of 0.62 and balanced accuracy score of 0.726. For the timeseries analysis, Simple , exponential and ARIMA models were forecasted and the weighted moving average shows the lowest 
residuals .
